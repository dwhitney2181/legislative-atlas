Sources and election result rules

This dashboard is a nonofficial research tool. It distinguishes live vote leaders (not called), publisher-reported apparent winners, explicit race calls, and certified election returns. A district without an evidence-backed result remains uncalled. Unknown or partially mapped districts remain neutral.

The statewide seat inventory is based on NCSL's 2026 chamber inventory. When available, legislative GIS data is drawn from Census-derived Election Wrld map files; the official 2025 U.S. Census files are the geographic reference. MultiState's 2026 election listings may provide publisher-labeled winners and prior party comparisons. Unofficial counts may come from civicAPI where covered. Some source webpages or API endpoints are incomplete or unavailable.

Only a recorded previous officeholder party can establish a flip. An open seat or retirement by itself is not a party flip. Seats in multi-member districts need distinct identity or source-level delegation totals; we never award all positions to a single candidate. All national totals refer only to the records actually loaded unless specifically identified as a full inventory.

API snapshots are cached for availability. The server can show the last observation if a feed fails; stale data are labeled and the observation time remains visible. Results are not automatically certified by complete precinct reporting.
